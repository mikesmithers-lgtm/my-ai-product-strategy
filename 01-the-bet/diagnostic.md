## Scores

### Contextual Moat — 4/5
*Workflow depth × switching cost. Would users leave in a weekend if a competitor showed up?*

**Score rationale:** Perform sits at the centre of how customers set objectives and run performance reviews. Leaving means migrating data, retraining managers and staff, relaunching the cycle, and renegotiating contracts. Launching across the wider suite adds further lock-in. It isn't a 5 yet because the split between contractual lock-in and real workflow dependency hasn't been tested.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):** Not yet named. Challengers have been seen, but the specific competitor from the partner challenge still needs to be recorded.

---

### Data Advantage — 3/5
*Proprietary signal that compounds with usage. What do you see that OpenAI doesn't?*

**Score rationale:** Perform builds up historical reviews, performance trends, and audit trails that become more useful the longer a customer uses it. A general AI tool could reproduce a one-off snapshot if a customer uploads documents, but it can't rebuild years of trend data or the audit history. The workflows are nuanced and embedded in organisations. The score is below the moat because a snapshot can be copied. It would move to 4/5 if customers can be shown to depend on this history for decisions such as calibration or succession, and couldn't easily recreate it.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):** Not yet named.

---

### Platform Exposure — 3/5
*Encroachment risk × pivot speed. If Apple/Google/OpenAI ships your hero feature native — then what?*

**Score rationale:** Basic goal-setting and review support is the kind of feature a platform could add to its assistant, so encroachment risk is real. The harder-to-copy value is in linking objectives and reviews to Learn and talent management, which a general assistant wouldn't have natively. The team could respond in about a quarter, which limits the damage from a basic feature launch but wouldn't match a platform that ships first. It would move to 4/5 if the Learn and talent links are deep enough that a platform would need a partnership or data access to match them.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):** Not yet named. If a platform is the likeliest threat, naming the specific one (Apple, Google, or OpenAI) would make this more concrete.

---

## Top Vulnerability
<!-- One line: what's the single biggest strategic risk? -->

Perform's core goal-setting and review workflow is easy for a platform to imitate, so its defensibility depends on the Learn and talent integration and the historical data that builds up over time.
